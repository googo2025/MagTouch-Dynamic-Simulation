Simulation-guided representation, refined with real source-device data

Both candidate models completed the prespecified plateau-training protocol and passed all six finite native replays and independent statistical checks. On two source-device batches of new complex paths excluded from candidate fitting, the selected model improved both localization and stability without a new XYZ output smoother. The target device mainly supports transfer of stability; no universal improvement across models, devices or legacy skills is claimed.

H0 is the already fully trained causal baseline. H1 adds multiscale history and real-position supervision. H2 uses the same input, capacity and data as H1 and adds motion-increment, reliable-hold and static-anchor constraints. H0 already uses history: the new representation is the difference between 48 current features and their causal exponential moving averages. It is neither averaging the XYZ output nor directly transferring virtual-trained weights. Causal input EMA is disclosed.
